## Latest: Offline Interpreter

<a href="https://github.com/rexzai1992/offline-interpreter"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rexzai1992/offline-interpreter/main/docs/assets/phones.png" width="100%" alt="Offline Interpreter on Android: the start-up screen, a live Mandarin and English conversation, and the Talk screen" /></a>

**[Offline Interpreter](https://github.com/rexzai1992/offline-interpreter)** is a two-person conversation translator that runs entirely on the phone. Each person just talks; speech recognition (SenseVoice and Whisper) and translation (Tencent Hy-MT2 on llama.cpp) happen on-device, with no internet and no account, in under 2 seconds from the last word to the translation. English, Malay, Japanese, Chinese and Filipino, in any pairing.

`React Native` `Expo` `llama.cpp` `Whisper` `On-device AI`
